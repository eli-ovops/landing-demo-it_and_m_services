# IT & M Services — internal visual strategy

## Business recognition

This is a Melbourne-wide mobile IT service, not a generic lifestyle or SaaS brand. The visual language therefore centres on careful device diagnosis, physical upgrades and the distinctive VHS-to-DVD service.

## Customer tone

Calm, practical and technically capable for residential and business clients. Avoid enterprise dashboards, generic work-from-home photography, loud “fix everything” promises, invented staff identity or fake repair outcomes.

## Image strategy and three visual high points

1. **Hero:** anonymous hands assessing an opened laptop: a specific computer-repair scene, not an office-stock image.
2. **Upgrade node:** laptop, SSD, memory and tools: a tactile representation of the verified upgrades/rebuilds service.
3. **VHS node:** tapes, capture device and disc case: a visually distinct, verified service that makes this business recognisable beyond routine repair.

## Information and CTA path

`Laptop trouble / we come to you` → specific repair range → upgrades/rebuilds → verified 2025 award → VHS conversion and broader support range → telephone or published-business-email CTA.

## Anti-template check before build

Replacing the name with a generic IT company would fail because the page is anchored to the distinct combination of Melbourne mobile support, on-device diagnosis, upgrades/rebuilds and VHS-to-DVD conversion. The former home-office hero, generic value cards, 255-review count and generic three-step flow are deliberately removed.
